AutoSetter 0.3.0 for macOS (Apple Silicon / Intel), Windows x64 and Linux x64 (AppImage / deb).

The first launch downloads and verifies the appropriate local Qwen 2.5 Instruct model automatically: 1.5B on computers with up to 12 GB RAM, 7B above 12 GB. Interrupted downloads can resume. Manual GGUF model selection remains available.

Activate the installed app with a purchased license or a card-backed trial key. Trial users receive 50 free results across all sessions. Before result 51, the app requests the agreed one-time 299 EUR payment. If the bank requires confirmation or the payment service is unavailable, the research pauses and can resume after payment. No subscription. The trial can be ended in the app before paid use.

The first activation permanently binds the key to that computer. After successful payment, the app verifies its signed lifetime activation locally without further licensing requests. Research still contacts Google and researched websites.

This release has no Developer ID notarization or Windows publisher signature. macOS and Windows may show installation warnings. Linux users may need to mark the AppImage executable or install the Debian package. SHA256SUMS.txt contains the installer checksums.

Copying an installer or activation certificate does not activate another computer.
